# Assets and licenses

## Pet illustrations and app icon (cute style)

Generated with Codex image generation for this project (character sheets in `macos/Tools/Art/`), then cut out and animated by PetForge into the GIFs in `macos/FocusNotch/Resources/Pets/cute_*.gif`.

## Pixel pets

Drawn procedurally by PetForge (`macos/Tools/PetForge/Sources/petforge/PixelPets.swift`). Original work.

## Notification chimes

`chime_break.wav`, `chime_focus.wav`, `chime_reminder.wav`, `chime_finish.wav` are synthesized by PetForge (`Sounds.swift`). Original work.

## Pet click sounds

All twelve are real animal recordings released under **Creative Commons 0** or into the **public domain** (<http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>): no attribution required, commercial use allowed. Each page's license was checked on 2026-10-20. Freesound clips use the public HQ MP3 previews, which carry the same license. Each clip was converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, trimmed to one call, faded in (10 ms) and out (60 ms), and normalized to -3 dBFS.

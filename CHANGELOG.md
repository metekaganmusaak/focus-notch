# Changelog

All notable changes to Focus Notch. Downloads are on the [Releases](https://github.com/metekaganmusaak/focus-notch/releases) page.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-20

### Added

- **Your own pet from images.** Pick up to four images, one per mood: waiting, working, sleeping and celebrating. Still images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, single-frame GIF, and HEIC on macOS) are turned into animated GIFs with the same breathing, typing, napping and hopping motion as the built-in pets. Animated GIFs are kept as they are. Moods you leave empty use the first image.
- **Remove solid background.** When an image sits on a flat color (a white or green backdrop, for example), that color is made transparent so your pet blends into the notch. You can turn it off for each pet.
- **Compact notch design** (Settings → General → Notch → Design). A narrower notch while a timer runs and smaller panels when it opens. A preview in Settings shows both states, and the notch opens for a moment when you switch so you can see it in place.

### Changed

- Clearer koala and duck sounds, from new CC0 recordings (see [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md)).
- **Add GIF…** is now **Add Pet…**. Dropping images on the Focus Friend tab opens them as a new pet.

### Fixed

- macOS: the Release build no longer crashes the Swift 6.3 compiler.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-20

### Added

- Every pet now comes in both styles: pixel versions of the hamster, penguin, owl, koala, duck and hedgehog.
- Every pet has a sound: the bunny, fox, panda, penguin, koala and hedgehog join the others (CC0 and public-domain recordings).
- Windows: choose a Small, Medium or Large notch so it fits a busy taskbar.

### Fixed

- Windows: the empty gap next to the pet is gone.
- Windows: the notch sometimes didn't appear at launch.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-20

First release of Focus Notch for macOS and Windows.
